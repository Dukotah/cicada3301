# Round 10B — Lane B3 — RESULTS

**Verdict: NEGATIVE.** Page-local semantic keys, derived the way the author demonstrably derives
them and spelled the way the author demonstrably spells them, do not decode any unsolved LP2
page. Both mandatory gates and all three positive controls passed, so this is a falsification and
not a broken harness.

All numbers below are reproducible: `cd liber-primus/analysis/round10b/B3-semantic-keys && for f
in gate.py t1_titlecrib.py t1b_period.py t1c_cribdrag.py t2_sweep.py novelty.py
structure_census.py; do PYTHONUTF8=1 python3 $f; done` (~4 min total).

**The single most important line in this lane:** `FIRFUMFERENFE` was *generated by the expander*
from the typed Latin word `CIRCUMFERENCE` — it was not in the input vocabulary. The
orthographic-orbit machinery reproduces the author's real, non-obvious spelling from scratch and

prior sweep in this repo lacked (`gp.keyword_to_indices` is a single greedy parse and can never

the key phase. Any keyword sweep that scores rigidly is blind to the author's own known solution.
`screen.py` (split at F positions, best key rotation per segment, length-weighted mean) fixes this
and is exact for a periodic key under any interrupter mask.

## 3. T1b — crib readout → **exact period** detection: **NEGATIVE** (`T1B.log`)

Replace the scorer with structure. If the page key is a repeating word of length L, a crib of
length m > L at the right offset yields a readout with `k[j] == k[j+L]` for all j. For a wrong
crib the readout is uniform noise, so an exact period over m−L constraints has probability
29^−(m−L) — for m=20, L=8 that is ~2·10⁻¹⁸. Any hit is unambiguous.

**Positive control (PASS):** cribbing `WELCOMEWELCOMEPILGRIM` at offset 0 of `03.jpg` produced
the readout

```
DIUINITYDIUINITYDIUIN        exact periods [(8, 13 constraints), (16, 5)]
```

— the key read straight out of the ciphertext with no key list involved.

**Real result:** 31 semantic opening phrases → 202 orthographic variants (≥12 runes) × 219
positions × 2 signs × interrupter-skip subsets = **168,452 readouts, 0 exact-period hits.**
NULL-A (pseudo-cribs) 0 hits. NULL-B (shuffled ct) 0 hits.
Best *partial* period fraction: real 0.800 / null-A 0.750 / null-B 0.750; means 0.1782 / 0.1790 /
0.1798 (sd ≈ 0.073) — the real corpus is indistinguishable from both nulls.

## 4. T1c — exhaustive crib **drag** with the same detector: **NEGATIVE** (`T1C.log`)

Removes the position guess entirely. Using the identity

```
key period L  ⟺  (ct[o+j] − ct[o+j+L]) ≡ (t[j] − t[j+L])  (mod 29)
```

the cipher sign and atbash both cancel, so the test is a plain pattern search of `E_L` inside
`D_L`, one vectorised numpy pass per (crib, period).

- **Positive control (PASS):** the drag re-finds `WELCOMEWELCOMEPILGRIM` at offset **0**, period
  **8**, 13 constraints, on `03.jpg`.
- **Real:** 202 crib variants × **every one of 12,956 offsets** × every period ≥ 2 →
  **0 hits**. NULL-A 0, NULL-B 0. Expected by chance across the whole drag: **0.0046**.

So: no semantic opening phrase from the author's own demonstrated vocabulary sits anywhere in the
unsolved stream under a repeating key of any length.

## 6. Structural finding: LP2 has **no header lines** (`STRUCTURE-CENSUS.log`)

The lane brief asked whether unsolved pages carry set-apart headers the way solved pages do.
Measured answer: **no.**

- Solved pages do: `03.jpg` first line = 7 runes (`WELCOME`), `14.jpg` = 5 runes (`A KOAN`),
  `05.jpg` = 10 runes (`SOME WISDOM`, plaintext).
- Across all 55 unsolved LP2 segments, **exactly one** has a short first line — seg 15, 9 runes,
  `FULMAEAYOEA` — and it is a **trailer, not a header**: it precedes the `&` then `$` ornament
  pair, closing the passage that began on seg 14. Every other segment opens with a full-width
  ~22-rune line (median 22.0, 12 lines/page).
- The LP2 title convention is shown by its one readable page (seg 56): the title is **inline and
  terminated by `.`** — `PARABLE.LIKE THE INSTAR TUNNELING TO THE SURFACE…`. This is why T1/T1b
  crib at sentence and ornament boundaries rather than at line starts.
- Ornament grammar: `&` ×18, `$` ×9 are the section marks; `§` ×1 closes seg 56.

**The "header keys its own body" construction therefore has no substrate in LP2.** That closes
the lane-brief handle by measurement rather than by sweep.

## 9. Scope limits — what this lane does NOT exclude

1. **Periodic keys only.** T1b/T1c detect a *repeating* key. A key used once (running key, pad,
   generator) leaves no period and is invisible to them. T2 likewise tiles its keys. Those
   families are excluded elsewhere in the repo, not here.
2. **Additive combiner.** The crib readout `k = ±(c − p)` assumes an additive/Beaufort-class
   combiner. A non-additive combiner (the ledger's no-repeat 29→28 form, ct-feedback, modular
   exponentiation) breaks the readout arithmetic.
3. **T1c assumes no interrupter null inside the crib window.** With observed F density ~1/35 and
   windows of 12–30 runes, roughly half of windows are clean; over 12,956 offsets that is ample
   but not total. T1b covers the skip subsets explicitly at its 219 positions.
4. **The candidate vocabulary is finite (126 words / 31 phrases).** It is the author's own
   demonstrated lexicon, but a semantic key drawn from a page's *unreadable* content remains
   formally uncheckable — the circularity the lane brief flagged is real and is not solved here.
   What T1c does is convert that circularity from "untestable" into "testable for any *phrase*
   we can guess", and no guessable phrase fits.

## 10. Residue worth someone's time

- The `screen.py` phase-flexible decoder found that **a rigid decode ranks the author's own key
  53rd** on a solved page. Any archived sweep in this repo that scored rigidly under a possible
  interrupter is suspect and cheap to re-run — that is a cross-cutting instrument issue, not a
  B3 result.
- The T1c drag identity (sign- and atbash-free period matching, one numpy pass) is a general
  crib tool. It would take minutes to point at non-semantic cribs, or at longer stretches of
  Runeglish function-word patterns, rather than at named phrases.
- Seg 15's `FULMAEAYOEA` trailer is the only short line in the unsolved corpus. It is 9 runes
  and it terminates a section. Nobody has decoded it because it is 9 runes of the same stream —
  but it is the one place in LP2 where the author's layout says "this is a discrete unit".
