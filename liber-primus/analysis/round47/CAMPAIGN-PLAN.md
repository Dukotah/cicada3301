## 2. Heavy-lift challenge specs (H1)

- **C7 Reference-corpus forge** (lead i, R46-P1): Gematria-Primus transliterations of non-English and archaic
  corpora (Greek, Hebrew, Old Norse, Middle English, Latin Vulgate, KJV-era verse…) with a stated
  transliteration table. Checker validates format, re-derives the transliteration from the declared table on a
  sample, and recomputes the 2-rune-word share and word-length profile against LP2.
- **C8 Non-English adjudicator** (L7-A): a GP-language scorer for one non-English register. Checker plants the
  submitter's held-out text at LP2 window sizes, measures detection power against the R36 panel's null, and
  requires a fixed power at a fixed false-positive rate.
- **C9 Second rejection-loop decoder** (L7-B): an alternative rejection-loop implementation that passes the C3
  gates and recovers plants generated by a loop variant the reference beam cannot represent.
- **C10 Attack under shuffled word lengths** (L1 (b′)): any statistic or attack with measured power on plants
  whose printed lengths are shuffled within sentence. Checker generates sealed plants + nulls and scores power.
