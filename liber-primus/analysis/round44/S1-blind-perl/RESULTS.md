## What this covers, and what it does not

Read as: excluded within (key space) x (transition model), with **no register conditional**.

1. **Key space:** every one of the 2^32 `srand` seeds, explicit or automatic; the idiom `int(rand(29))`; no draws consumed before the first rune; the stream starts at rune 0 of page 0.
2. **Transition model:** one draw per rune; a rejection consumes one draw or two, with or without the in-stream coin; at most 8 / 12 / 24 rejections in the first 64 / 120 / 262 runes; both signs of the key. **Any constant in the combiner, Atbash and Beaufort are covered**, which no earlier sweep in this repository can say.
3. **Register:** none. Any plaintext whose symbols have IoC x 29 of about 1.4 or more over the page, in any language, spelling or numbering of the symbols.

Not covered: a warm-up (draws consumed before rune 0); a stream that starts at another section or page head; other reductions of `rand` (`rand()*29` rounded, `rand(28)+1`, a modulus of a larger draw); a key pointer that stalls (an F left in clear); rejection by deleting or rewriting the rune without a draw; separators that consume draws; Perl builds whose `rand` is not `drand48` (none known on Linux).

Reopens if: any of the items above is given a reason; or a plaintext model with near-uniform symbol use is proposed.
