## What this covers, and what it does not

1. **Key space:** glibc `srand(t)` for every t in [0, 2^24) and every second of 2011-12-01 .. 2014-04-03 UTC; two reductions; no draws before the first rune; stream starts at rune 0 of page 0.
2. **Transition model:** as lane S1 (one or two draws per rejection, in-stream coin, both key signs, any combiner constant, Atbash and Beaufort).
3. **Register:** none needed.

Not covered: seeds outside the two ranges (`time ^ pid`, `time * pid`, microseconds, the rest of 2^32); other reductions; other C libraries (musl, BSD, MSVC `rand` differ); a warm-up; a start at another section head; a stalled key pointer; deletion or rewrite without a draw. The full 2^32 for both reductions is about ten core-hours with this kernel and is the natural next run.
