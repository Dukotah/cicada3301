# AUDIT: what in this repo could inhibit a solve (read-only audit, 2026-10-30)

Method: shell greps, file reads and small JSON queries of `LEDGER.json` only. No heavy Python, no git writes, no filesystem-wide find. The one `find` ran inside the repo to look for orphan `.pyc` files. Not run, so unverified: `verify_capsule.py`, CI itself, the GitHub PR state (#7-#10), the contents of the Round 36 O2/O3 lanes, and the natural-rate power of the Round 27 `grind27` stage-A screen. Every other claim carries a citation. Line numbers are as of this checkout.

## Verdict

The doctrine and the ledger discipline are sound. The damage is in the layers that sit around them and were never brought up to the doctrine.

1. **The front-door oracle is the L7-A defect.** `verify_solution.py` and `PROBLEM.json` still adjudicate by an English-only -5.5 band using rigid and legacy-beam decoders. A true Latin, Old English, vowel-dropped or `skip_by_two` solve would be told FAIL by the oracle the README and AGENTS.md tell strangers to trust.
2. **The hit predicate cannot run.** `hitfn20` is the predicate of record. It imports the Round 19 nine-register panel (`round19/I2/models/panel.npz`), whose Welsh corpus is unrecoverable. That `models/` directory does not exist in this checkout. Its bar was fitted on the legacy beam at L=120 only.
3. **Several foreclosures outlive the evidence that was meant to support them.**
   - The ledger's top "Do NOT re-run" block forbids "PRNG keystreams" and "another keytext" as absolutes.
   - The Round 20 and 21 plans say keytexts are foreclosed "by mechanism". The README and PAPER say that claim was retracted, and R26-B ran the lane.
   - Round 1's `DEAD_ENDS.md` still says skip-encipherment must not be revived. That model is the repo's core decoder today.
   - The Py2.7-MT S1 sweep is written up as "EXHAUSTED / finished / Closed; needs nobody" (R7 violation). Its panel cannot be rebuilt and its power was measured with English plants only.
4. **The ledger does not index the lanes most likely to be re-run.** Rounds 1-7, 9, 11, 14 and 15 have no `LEDGER.json` rows, yet their "Do NOT re-run" lines are the ones agents obey. Only 7 of 60 negative or eliminated rows carry structured `three_conditionals`.
5. **Tooling caps would silently truncate a 12-register world.**
   - `beam_v3.PANEL_PATH` is pinned to the nine-register panel.
   - `ROW_DTYPE` has a 9-wide `z` field, and `validate_row` defaults to 9 registers.
   - `validate_ledger.check_r3_store` imports the Round 19 `adjudicate`.
   - `grind27.c` hard-codes `NREG 9`.
   - Round 36 B2 already labels its rows "nine-register" (`round36/B2-php-awake/sweep.py:200`).
6. **Live state is buried under frozen "current" banners and wrong counts.** The ledger has 202 rows. The nav docs say 142, 162, 172 and 181. AGENTS.md section 8 still says "nothing is running" and lists tasks that were since done.

Several lanes are cheap to reopen under the repaired instrument; see "Reopen candidates" below. The B-04 / KDF / PRNG families and the Round 11 number-channel lenses are the best ones.
