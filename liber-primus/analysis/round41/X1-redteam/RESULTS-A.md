## 1. I1 -- what the numbers say

- **Population.** The right null for "a bar applied to sweep survivors" is the survivors of wrong keys on the real head, decoded with the real tail; shuffled-LP2 wrong keys (null12) are less English-like at the head the screen selected (median 2.33 vs 2.81). I1's population matches the real sweeps at KS p 0.5-0.7 on 131,786 rows, which is about the strongest check available. It is not circular: seeds are blind to real rows, and the screen threshold (`frozen_op12.json`) was frozen on plant/null calibration in Round 37, before any of this.
- **Tail.** Exponential POT line vs the data (keyskip1 / keyskip2, 307,377 pooled real rows): above 5.0 observed 1,459 / 1,534 vs 1,878 / 2,003 expected; 5.5: 254 / 279 vs 548 / 591; 6.0: 32 / 38 vs 160 / 175; 6.5: 2 / 6 vs 47 / 52; 7.0: 1 / 1 vs 13.6 / 15.2. The real tail is cut off near 7.1 (GPD xi about -0.14, implied endpoint about 7.1; the observed max is 7.098). An exponential bar therefore overstates the 1 % level by roughly 2.7 at N=131,786 and 3.5 at N=1e6. Safe for flagging, lossy for sensitivity (#12). The pooled real sample is not independent (R40-B1's 4.0 / 4.1 cells re-decode R37-I2 seeds), which makes the counts above slightly optimistic about the sample size, not about the direction.
- **Honest +- at N=1e6 (L=120):** statistical 0.09 (bootstrap, 100 resamples: 0.082 / 0.085); model band about 7.1 to 10.5; at L=240 statistical 0.22-0.24 and the null max is 6.16 vs bar 10.05-10.35. State the bar as "upper bracket".

## 2. B2 -- what the numbers say

The alignment is right: a 5-cell wrapper check against an independent generator, 10 independent plants (9 recovered, the loss a screen miss on a with-skip head), and the controls re-scored under the bar of record all agree with B2. B2's sweeps are valid negatives for their cells at power equal to the control (39-40 of 40, EN_MODERN and EN_NOVOWEL). What was wrong was the bar (null12), the denominator (LP64 only), and the scope of the misaligned control: the finding that offset-0 sweeps had partial reach at a shifted key is real and larger than stated (offset 1 under keyskip1: 10/15), but it is relation-specific, and it means B2's cells (1,-1) and (2,-1) re-covered ground the earlier sweeps partly saw; the genuinely new cells are (0,+1) and offset 3 onward (offset 3 needs three start skips, beyond the screen's k=2).
