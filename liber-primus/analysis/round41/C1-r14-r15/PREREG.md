## 5. Decision rule (fixed now)
- alpha = **0.0025** per (family, level): 0.01 family-wise over {R14, R15} x {W, P} (Bonferroni over 4 tests). With D=1000 the smallest attainable p is 0.000999, so alpha is attainable.
- For each (family, level, statistic) all **three p-values** (block 100 / 250 / 1000) are reported. The **decision p is the maximum of the three** (the most structure-retaining null binds: round40 X1 showed R13 moved 0.004 -> 0.070).
- A (family, level) CROSSES iff BOTH statistics (Z-family and raw) have decision p <= alpha. Otherwise it is NEGATIVE (if the control passed) at that level. Level W is the primary verdict for the cells; level P is secondary and reported as page-localised evidence; a P-only crosser is still flagged-for-oracle (a page-local hit is not excluded by a W-level null).
- A crosser is NEVER called solved: it goes to a flagged-for-oracle bundle `ORACLE-<id>/` per HUMAN-ORACLE.md and the ledger row stays open with a flagged-for-oracle note.
- Reported but non-binding: the best observed pmax against the survivor-population bar of record (`bars_v3 family="survivor12"`, 10.46 at N=1e6); that bar is for key-sweep survivors and is NOT the judge for these direct readings.
- No threshold changes after seeing a result; later changes only as a dated addendum file.

## 6. Positive controls (SAME pipeline, SAME streams; a family whose control fails is `inconclusive`, not negative)

Gate: a (family, level, register) cell passes iff 100% of its plants are recognised; positive_control = passed only if all cells pass, else failed with counts.
