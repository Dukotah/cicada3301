## U3 - the composed rule (as written in PREREG section 3)
**The rule.** *Words are the map*: the codebook is the LP1 word list W[0..725] in document order (each entry has a Gematria value). *Meaning is the road*: ciphertext word k (boundaries = the cleartext '-' and '.' interrupters only; '/' is a line wrap) reaches codebook entry e_k = W[(s + p_k) mod 726], where the pointer is (rho1) p_k = k, the word's position (**literal**: words are read against the map in order), (rho2) p_k = r_k, the rune offset of the word start, or (rho3) p_k = l_k, the word length. *Numbers are the direction*: d_k = value(e_k) mod M (M = 29 or 28) is applied at each word boundary as a signed (sigma = +-1) advance: (A1) additive shift of word k, (A2) cumulative shift sum_{j<=k} d_j, (A3) key-pointer advance through K0 = the LP1 rune stream (the map) decoded through the keyskip beam. **The most literal composition is rho1 x A3 with M = 29 and s = 0**; A1/A2 are the "additive shift" alternative the plan names; rho2/rho3 and M = 28, s = 1..28, sigma are alternatives/convenience parameters. A pointer from the ciphertext word's own gematria sum was EXCLUDED before the run (not a well-formed cipher: the encipherer would need a fixed point that does not exist for ~1/3 of words). The semantic ("dictionary meaning") reading of "meaning" is not operationalized at all - positional/length stand-ins only; this is the biggest gap between the clue and what was tested.
**Control.** 24/24 cells planted with the independent encipherer in `enciphers_u2_u3.py` and recovered: min L=120 pmax 16.13, recovery240 >= 0.9875 (A1/A2 are exact inversions so recovery is 1.000 by construction; their value is the pmax/bar check and the decoder plumbing; A3 recovery goes through the real beam and `enc_exact`/`enc_pair`).
**Result.** N = 1,044 (ks1) / 1,044 (ks2) / 2,088 (direct A1+A2). Best ks1 4.484 (rho1/M29/A3, page 0, HEBREW) vs 6.632 / 9.235; best ks2 4.660 (rho2/M28/A3, page 1, EN_KJV) vs 6.529 / 9.034; best direct 5.085 (rho3/M29/A2, page 2, CY) vs 6.895 / 9.235 (keyskip1 bar used - conservative). The literal composition (rho1, A3, M29, s=0, both signs, 3 pages, ks1+ks2) best 3.035. Flagged: none. Near-miss: none.
**Three conditionals.** key space: the 348 x 3-page grid above; transition model: A1/A2 direct (no key stream, no skip relation), A3 keyskip1/keyskip2 beam on K' indexed by cipher position; adjudicator: panel-R36 with EN_MODERN-only control.
**not_covered.** semantic/dictionary meaning; pointer from the ciphertext word's gematria; alternative codebooks (sentences/lines, other books, the unsolved text itself); k not restarting beyond the page-0 head; atbash; s > 28; other M; other composition orders (direction-then-road); pages > 2 as heads; free relation.

## Controls: what they do and do not show
They show the pipeline (encipherer -> decoder -> panel -> bars) recovers each planted construction with large margin (planted pmax 14-31 vs bars 9.0-9.5; null mean 2.3), for EN_MODERN plaintext, at L=120/240, with natural doublet-skip rates for the beam variants. They do not show that a true LP2 key in these families would be found for a non-English plaintext (power by register is not re-measured here), nor for a construction that differs from the planted one (e.g., a different base key). The encipherers and decoders are separate code (key building from plaintext/cipher word lengths vs from ciphertext), but they share the LP1 data and sequence definitions by necessity.
