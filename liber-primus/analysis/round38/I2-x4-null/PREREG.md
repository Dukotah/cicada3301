## What X4 is (read from `round22/C-literal-imperatives/imperatives.py::sweep_x4` and `round37/C3/rescore.py`)
For the stream S (LP2 pages 0-54, 12,956 runes): K[i] = f(S[i]) mod 29 with f in {prime value, prime index (i+1), totient (p-1)} - the key is a per-letter function of the CIPHERTEXT stream itself; letters = S or atbash(S); sign -1/+1: 3 x 2 x 2 = 12 configs. C3 decoded 6 page-start windows (pages 0,10,20,30,40,50) at L=120 and L=240 under beam_v3 panel_max keyskip1 + keyskip2 (panel-R36, 12 registers) = 72 rows/relation/L, and judged against bars TRANSFERRED from random-key nulls (`round36/B1-orbit-12reg/null12.json`, actual-N / declared 1e6). That transfer is the thing under test.

## The bespoke null
For each of >= 2,000 draws j: S_j = doublet-matched shuffle of LP2 (`dfshuffle`, <= 86 adjacent equal pairs, seed 3301+j; identical routine to C3). RE-DERIVE K_j = f(S_j) for all 12 configs exactly as above, decode the same 6 window starts x keyskip1/2 at L=120 (and L=240 as budget allows; stated if fewer draws), adjudicate under panel-R36 (`AD.adjudicate`). Record per draw and relation the FAMILY MAX of pmax over non-degenerate rows (same degenerate filter as C3: mds <= 6 or IoC*N >= 4.24 -> excluded), plus the row-level pmax pool. Configs whose decode is a constant independent of the stream (prime_index/none/sign-1 and prime_index/atbash/sign+1: plain = -1 identically) are verified constant on 20 draws and then counted as degenerate without re-decoding (declared).
Bar: empirical q99 of the family max at the family's N (60 non-degenerate rows/rel/L), with a Gumbel fit to the 2,000 maxima as a cross-check. Row p-value = fraction of null family-maxima >= the row's pmax (family-wise).

## Planted control (run FIRST)
The family's construction: key derived from the ciphertext. Plain = g(C) with g(c) = letter(c) + sign*f(c) mod 29 (letter = c or atbash c). To plant, choose C_i = g^-1(P_i) (random preimage if several) so the ciphertext's OWN derived key deciphers it exactly; then run the family's decoder pipeline unchanged (K = f(C), beam_v3 keyskip1/2 at the family sign, L=120 and L=240). Facts measured: g is a bijection only for prime_index/none/sign+1 and prime_index/atbash/sign-1; the prime-value and totient configs have |image| 20-21 of 29 (an arbitrary plaintext is NOT enciphered by them; only plaintexts over the image alphabet are), and the other two prime_index configs map everything to one constant. Control design: (a) full gate on the 2 bijective configs - plants from `testhalves12.npz` EN_MODERN/LATIN/GREEK, 2 plants each, both relations; PASS = recovery >= 0.90 for every cell (>= 11 of 12); (b) supplementary for the 8 image-limited configs: plaintext symbols outside the image are replaced by the nearest in-image symbol (documented distortion, recovery measured against the distorted truth). The (b) result is reported but does not gate; a config with (b) failing is reported as "decoder cannot carry it", not negative-by-silence. Gate (a) fails -> lane is inconclusive and no re-judgement is made.

Filter audit: list EVERY real X4 row removed by the filter (mds <= 6 or IoC*N >= 4.24) with its raw pmax, mds, IoC*N, preg, and whether it clears the bespoke bar; a degenerate decode that clears is reported as a RECOGNIZER FAILURE (constant stream scored by an LM), not a hit. A non-degenerate row that clears -> L=240 re-screen if not already, then oracle bundle (coordinator).
