########## dieharder — CAVEAT ##########
dieharder needs ~1e8-1e11 bits. We have 6.3e4. It REWINDS the file and
re-uses the same bits, which INVALIDATES the p-values (they become
measures of self-consistency, not randomness). Reported for completeness
only, and ONLY as REAL-vs-control comparisons on identical rewind counts.
