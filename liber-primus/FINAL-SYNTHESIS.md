  pad), bioinformatics approximate-repeat finding — all null. *(iters 2, 6)*
- **Smirnov/Carlitz deterministic rewrite** — gate-validated, real sweep pinned to its random control. *(iter 11)*
- **Message-existence is undecidable from the ciphertext** — LP2 sits inside *both* the English-under-pad

- **The external key (AN-END deep-web page)** is not the hash of any held object across SHA-2, SHA-3,

- **Artifact premises audited clean:** the ciphertext transcription (krisyotam ≡ relikd char-for-char over
