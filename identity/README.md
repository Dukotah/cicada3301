**Bottom line: unknown.** No source has ever credibly confirmed who created or runs Cicada 3301. This folder weighs the theories; it does not claim an answer.

Populated by `research/04-identity-theories.md` and the adversarial verification pass.

## Naive-outsider attribution sweep — 2026-07-29 (skill-stack / design-community lens)

New angle: instead of hunting WHO via keys/stylometry/timelines (all sealed dead),
ask what the artifact's total DESIGN reveals about the maker's skill-stack, and who
in the ARG/puzzle-DESIGN community (not solvers) had that intersection circa 2011–14.

- **Adrian Hon / Six to Start — the closest real skill-match, and he rules himself
  out (credible).** Hon's studio built the transmedia ARG for **BBC Two's *The Code*
  (2011)** — hosted by mathematician Marcus du Sautoy, built explicitly around
  **prime numbers and cicada gestation cycles**, six months *before* Cicada 3301.
  He also led **Perplex City** (Mind Candy), a physical-artifact treasure-hunt ARG.
  That is the exact ARG+crypto+physical-dead-drop+community-coordination skill-stack
  Cicada demands. Conspiracy DMs regularly accuse him; he **publicly and consistently
  denies involvement**, calling the timing "a big coincidence" and noting his ARGs are
  *funded/for-profit* whereas Cicada's funding is opaque (mssv.net, 2020; HN 25581382,
  2020). Reliability: **credible denial.** Value: establishes the "cicada + primes"
  motif was already in ARG culture in 2011 (plausible *influence* source, not authorship),
  and that the obvious professional candidate self-excludes on-record — narrows toward
  a NON-commercial maker (matches the no-monetization disqualifier for a commercial ARG).
- **Nox Populi (DEF CON 26 Crypto & Privacy Village talk, "Cicada: What Puzzles Can
  Teach Us About Cryptography", 2018).** A self-described solver, NOT an author; the
  talk is a solver's crypto/privacy retrospective, not an insider construction reveal.
  A GitHub gist (nkwgrbibtkfnsvsn) technically debunks her "2012 winner" claim by
  showing her PGP artifact is a message *addressed to* 3301 (recipient key-ID = 3301),
  not *from* it. Nets to zero on authorship. Reliability of debunk: credible (verifiable
  by anyone via the base64 key-ID). Do not treat Nox Populi as an insider source.
- **"Cicada 3301 finally identified" (cicada3301solvedcom.wordpress, 2023) = LARP.**
  Rests solely on a 2018 unsigned "Deprogramming" video; NO PGP, NO names, NO method.
  Refuted — folds into the standing unsigned-post problem (7A35090F is the only gold
  standard). Ignore.
- **2014 winners' cohort:** never went public; no member has described how LP was built.
  Dead end (fandom "2013 Winners Questions" page returns HTTP 402, not retrievable).
- **Design fingerprint corroborated externally:** the community independently observes
  the LP2 doublet deficit (community reads it as autokey/Playfair-class). Our repo went
  further and *refuted* autokey (Campaign X), pinning it to a soft ~83% no-repeat OTP
  filter — i.e. the maker built a NON-textbook hardening the community still
  mis-labels. That the maker out-designed the standard cipher taxonomy is itself a
  skill-stack signal (bespoke construction, not a named classical cipher).

**Net for GOAL 2:** No new named creator. The freshest defensible narrowing is
*negative-space*: the one professional whose skill-stack is a near-perfect match
(Adrian Hon) self-excludes credibly, and the no-monetization + opaque-funding profile
points away from the commercial ARG-studio world toward a non-commercial
cypherpunk/privacy maker (or small collective) who treated ARG craft as a vehicle,
not a business. Consistent with, and does not overturn, the prior "cypherpunk
collective" verdict. Persistent-operator verifiable loose end: the two unidentified
high-entropy blobs (PICKUP-HERE T2/T3) remain the only artifact-side unknowns.
