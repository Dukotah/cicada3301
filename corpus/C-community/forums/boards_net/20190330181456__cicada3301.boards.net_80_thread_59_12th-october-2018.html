There are a few who work on this when time allows.
I find the best place to discuss is through the IRC network, that's where you will find the people who have dedicated the most time over many years.
But remember: "patience is a virtue", IRC can be a slow burner, especially for a puzzle that has had no new ideas for years.
My advice is:
1) Journey through the shitposting to find the right channels,
2) Ask your questions and then stay connected 24/7, we all have real life to contend with, we all live in different time-zones, etc. But we do try and help as much as possible when available.
3) using a ping can help you cut through the noise to talk to someone directly
