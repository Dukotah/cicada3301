I've been following 3301's recruitment since the first round. I must first divulge I was a Freemason. I wanted to share details of a dream I recently had involving the Cicada group. I was at a drinks party and a particular group beckoned me over. A middle aged woman began to question my interest in Cicada. She was particularly interested when I said I wasn't any good at decryption, but as a former Mason I was intrigued by the philosophy. The woman held my wrist firmly, a feeling of intense euphoria washing over me. I fainted and awoke strapped into a machine which I believe was some sort of centrifuge. I quickly passed out again. I then awoke in different clothing on a coastal resort with two upper middle class men who were quite menacing. They were telling me how they were looking forward to putting me through a ritual called "The Plates". 
The dream ended there. I thought I'd share it as I had similar dreams relating to Masonry. Very powerful, lucid dreams. Perhaps the result of prolonged, persistent thought about deeper subjects other than work or what's on the television?
